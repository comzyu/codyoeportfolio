# Poto — the two signature features

**Same Time** and **Pass the Roll**. Written 18 Nov 2026 from Cody's brief. These are the features meant to make Poto spread on its own, so each is specified end to end: what it is, how it feels, every step, the edge cases, what it shares, how it grows, how it earns.

Both use what Poto already has: the Poto camera, film looks, developing, postcards, signature stamps, the share renderer. Neither needs a camera roll or AI.

---

## Why these two

Most photo apps are a feed of friends' pictures. That isn't a reason to invite anyone. These two features **can't be done alone**: Same Time needs friends in other places at the same moment, Pass the Roll needs someone to pass to. Both finish with **one beautiful image that every person in it wants to post**. That's the loop:

```
make one with friends ─▶ needs more people ─▶ invite (they must install)
        ▲                                              │
        │                                              ▼
  non-users see it ◀── everyone in it posts the same collage to IG / TikTok
```

Each finished collage is posted by **several people at once**, to several different audiences, every one carrying the poto mark. One group, many ads.

---

# 1. Same Time

> One moment. Everyone you love, wherever they are. One frame each.

### The idea
A group picks a moment, or Poto picks one by surprise. At that exact instant, everyone in the group gets the same prompt, wherever they are in the world. Each person has a few minutes to shoot **one frame** with the Poto camera. It's 9pm in Singapore, 2pm in London, 6am in LA: one friend is at dinner, one at work, one still in bed. Nobody sees anyone else's frame until the moment closes. Then every frame **develops together** and becomes one collage, made to post in 9:16 and 4:5, with everyone's name and local time in the corner.

The magic is the contrast: the same second, completely different lives.

### Who it's for
- Long-distance friends and family across time zones (the strongest case)
- Friend groups in one city ("what's everyone doing at 6pm Friday")
- Couples (two-frame Same Time)
- Inside events ("everyone, 9:00, first dance")

### Setting one up
1. **Home → + → Same Time.**
2. **Name it** (optional): "the group chat", "sunday dinner".
3. **Choose friends:** 2 to 12 people. Friends not on Poto get an invite link. They must install Poto to join; their slot waits as "invited".
4. **Choose when:**
   - **At a set time:** pick date and time. Everyone sees it in their own local time ("Fri 9:00 PM your time · 2:00 PM for Dana").
   - **Every week:** a repeating moment, e.g. every Sunday 9pm.
   - **Surprise me:** Poto picks a random moment within a window you set ("sometime Saturday afternoon, my time"). Nobody knows exactly when. That's the BeReal thrill, kept to your own group.
5. **Choose the look:** one film look for everyone, recommended (the collage looks like one roll), or "everyone's own".
6. **Choose how long people have:** 1, 3 (default) or 10 minutes.
7. **Choose when it develops:**
   - **Right after the window closes:** instant reveal.
   - **At the end of the day:** the default, as Cody described; a nightly reveal.
   - **Next morning:** matches Poto's overnight developing.

### The moment itself
- **Before:** a quiet countdown on Home ("Same Time with Dana, Mei +3 · in 2 h 14 min"). No nagging.
- **At the instant:** everyone gets the notification at once: **"Same Time. Now."** The Live Activity / lock screen shows the countdown of the window.
- **Opening it goes straight to the camera**, already loaded with the group's film look and a thin bar showing the seconds left. One shutter press. One frame. No retakes (that's the honesty). The frame drops in undeveloped.
- **While the window runs:** a row of the group's signature stamps; each lights up when that person has shot. You see *that* they shot, never *what*.
- **Late:** up to 30 minutes after, still allowed, marked "late · +12 min" on their tile. After that the slot closes.
- **Missed:** the slot doesn't break the collage. It shows a quiet blank frame with their local time and city: *"asleep · 3:12 AM · Seoul"* (if it was night for them) or *"missed · 2:40 PM · London"*. These blanks are part of the charm: the collage shows who was asleep on the other side of the world.

### The reveal
At develop time, everyone gets **"Your Same Time has developed."** Opening it plays the reveal: all frames sit side by side as blank emulsion, then come up together, light areas first, colour last (the developing effect we already have), each tile labelled with name, city and local time. Then the finished collage settles.

From there:
- **Share** (the main action): Story 9:16 or Post 4:5, saved or sent straight to Instagram Stories or TikTok photo mode.
- **Flip any frame** to its postcard back, where that person can add a short handwritten note *after* the reveal ("ignore the pyjamas").
- **Keep:** the collage goes into each person's calendar on that day and can be put on their desk.

### The collage (the part that spreads)
This is what gets posted, so it gets the most design care.
- **Layouts for every group size, 2 to 12,** in both 9:16 and 4:5. Each layout is designed, not auto-tiled: 2 is a pair, 3 a staggered row, 4 a grid, 5–6 a contact sheet, 7–12 a dense grid.
- **Each tile carries,** tiny and minimal in a corner: their name or @handle, their **local time** and **city** (e.g. *dana · 2:04 PM · London*). Optionally their signature stamp in miniature.
- **The header line** tells the story in one glance: *18 Nov · the same second in 5 cities* or *9:00 PM SGT · 4 time zones*.
- **The poto mark** sits on the frame, never on a photo (Cody's rule).
- **Templates:** Contact sheet, White frames, Film strip, Postcard pile. Free gets two; Plus gets all of them and new ones each season.
- **Respects Instagram's safe zones** in 9:16 (the share renderer already does).
- **Everyone in the group gets the same collage** and is nudged once, gently: "Share your Same Time?" Five people posting the same image is five audiences seeing poto.

### Rules, safety and edge cases
- **Time zones and daylight saving:** stored as one UTC instant; displayed per person. Recurring moments follow the creator's local time and say so.
- **Notifications off:** Poto asks for notification permission when you join a Same Time (that's when it makes sense). Without it, the slot still opens in-app and the moment shows on Home; they'll likely be late or miss it.
- **Someone joins after the moment was set:** fine, they're in from the next one.
- **Someone leaves:** their past frames stay in past collages unless they remove them; future slots drop them.
- **A frame someone doesn't want in the collage:** any member can hide **their own** frame before sharing; the creator can remove a member. Report and block work as everywhere.
- **Location:** city-level only, from the shot; anyone can turn location off ("somewhere · 2:04 PM").
- **Group size:** 2–6 free, up to 12 with Plus (layouts above 12 stop being readable).

### How it grows Poto
- **You need friends to do it,** and the best version (different cities) needs friends far away.
- **Invites can't be skipped:** a slot for someone not on Poto only fills when they install.
- **Every reveal produces one ready-made post,** shared by several people at once, each with the poto mark.
- **Recurring moments bring people back every week** without streaks or guilt.
- **Things to measure:** invites sent per Same Time, % of members who shoot in the window, collages shared per Same Time, installs from invites.

### How it earns
- **Free:** groups up to 6, one weekly moment, two templates.
- **Poto+:** up to 12 people, several recurring moments, all templates, choose the window length, early access to seasonal templates.
- **Poto+ Events:** Same Time inside an event, for all guests (the "everyone, right now" moment of a wedding).

# 2. Pass the Roll

> One roll of film. It travels from friend to friend. Nobody sees a frame until it's finished.

### The idea
Someone starts a roll and passes it on. Each person shoots their share of frames with the Poto camera, then passes the roll to the next. Nobody can see what's on it. When the last frame is shot, the roll **rewinds and develops for everyone at the same moment**, as one shared contact sheet, every frame credited. It's a disposable camera passed around your friends, across days and cities.

### Who it's for
- Friends who live apart ("a week in each of our lives")
- A trip where everyone takes turns
- Long-distance couples (a roll that goes back and forth)
- A slow birthday present: a roll passed through someone's friends, revealed on their birthday

### Starting a roll
1. **Rolls → Start a roll to pass.**
2. **Name it:** "summer 26", "for mum's 60th".
3. **Film look and roll size:** 12, 24 or 27 frames (36 with Plus). The canister on the shelf takes the look's colours.
4. **Who it goes to:**
   - **In order:** pick people and drag them into order.
   - **Open:** each person chooses who gets it next. More surprising and spreads further; it can travel to friends-of-friends.
5. **Frames each:** set automatically (frames ÷ people) or by hand; the starter can keep the last few frames to "close" the roll.
6. **Time each person gets:** 24 hours by default (12 h / 48 h / 1 week).
7. **Develops:** the moment the last frame is shot, at a set time ("on her birthday, 8am"), or next morning.

### Holding the roll
- **The roll arrives** as a canister landing in your camera, with a sound and a haptic tick. Notification: *"Mei passed you the roll · frames 7–12 are yours."*
- **The camera** shows a counter for **your** frames only ("3 of 6 left"). Previous frames are invisible: no peeking. That's the point.
- **The canister's label** shows the roll's journey so far, as postmarks: *Singapore → Osaka → Seoul*, plus the names of everyone who has held it.
- **When your frames are done:** **Pass the roll.** In an ordered roll it goes to the next person; in an open roll you pick a friend, or invite someone new by link (they must install Poto to receive it). The canister leaves your camera with a small handoff animation.

### If someone stalls
- A gentle reminder at half time, one more near the end.
- When their time runs out, the starter chooses: **give them another day**, **skip them** (their frames go to the next person), or **take the roll back**.
- If someone leaves Poto or deletes their account, the roll skips them automatically.
- The starter can **finish early** at any time; unused frames are simply not on the sheet.

### The reveal
When the last frame is shot, every holder gets: *"Your roll is complete. Developing now · ready at 8:00."* At develop time, the reveal plays for everyone at once: the roll rewinds, comes out of the canister as a strip, and every frame develops in order, each credited (*frames 7–12 · Mei · Osaka*).

Then:
- **The contact sheet:** the whole roll as one image, in 9:16 and 4:5, with names, cities, the roll's name and the journey line (*Singapore → Osaka → Seoul → London · 12 days*). This is the post.
- **Every frame becomes a postcard** that its shooter can write a note on.
- **Everyone keeps the roll:** a canister on their Rolls shelf with all the holders' names on the label, and a folder of the prints.
- **A roll stamp:** everyone who held it collects a stamp of that roll for their desk. Only the people who were on the roll can have it.

### Rules and edge cases
- **One roll at a time per holder** (free); several active rolls with Plus.
- **Privacy:** only holders see the roll and the sheet. Sharing the sheet publicly is each person's choice, and anyone can hide their own frames before it's shared.
- **Content:** report and block as everywhere; the starter can remove a frame from the shared sheet.
- **Time zones:** the journey and timestamps use each holder's local time.
- **Offline:** shots are kept on the phone and upload when back online; the roll can't be passed until uploaded.

### How it grows Poto
- **A roll can't finish without passing it,** and passing it to someone new means they install Poto.
- **Open rolls travel past your own friends,** to friends of friends.
- **The finished sheet is shared by everyone on it,** with the journey line making people ask "how did you do that?"
- **Things to measure:** average holders per roll, % of rolls finished, new installs per roll, sheets shared per roll.

### How it earns
- **Free:** one active passing roll at a time, up to 27 frames, standard looks.
- **Poto+:** several rolls at once, 36-frame rolls, all film looks, set-a-date reveals (the birthday surprise).
- **Seasonal limited canisters:** a Winter roll, a Lunar New Year roll. Buy once, pass it around; everyone on it collects its stamp.
- **Poto+ Events:** a roll passed around the guests of an event.

## Signature stamps (supporting both)
Not the headline, but they make both features feel collected. Each tile in a Same Time collage and each credit on a passed roll can show the person's signature stamp in miniature, and finishing either gives everyone a stamp for their desk.
