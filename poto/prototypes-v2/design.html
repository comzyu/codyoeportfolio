Design direction one

From the 49 references you picked: real paper and stamps, photo calendars, frosted glass, editorial type with lots of space, warm grain. Three parts to react to: colour, type, and the embossed postcard back.

What your picks share

Six of them, to show the threads. Paper you could touch; one photo as the hero; glass that is barely there; big quiet type next to a hand-written line; warm grain instead of flat colour.

Colour · your pick: Darkroom

Never pure white, never pure black. Light is a warm paper white; dark is a deep warm charcoal with a hint of brown, like a darkroom. The photos stay the only strong colour; the one accent is a postmark red, used for tiny things only.

Type · your pick: Pinyon Script

A clean modern sans for everything you read and tap, with a script for the human moments: names, a month, a note. The contrast is what feels premium. Three script options; the sans stays Inter for now (or SF Pro, Apple's own, in the app).

The embossed back

A detailed artwork across the whole back, pressed into the paper with no ink, quieter under the writing so the note stays clear. Move your mouse or finger over a card to move the light, like tilting it. Each country carries both marks: a thin strip of colour at the bottom and a small wax seal.

How it works: each pattern is a height map (white is raised, grey is flat). The light is calculated per pixel from it, the same way the 3D postcard will do it, so your own designs drop straight in (see the design spec: 2048 × 2896, 50% grey = flat). The artworks are public-domain prints from the Art Institute of Chicago's open-access collection (Hokusai, Meryon, a songket textile), turned into height maps. They show the effect; the final artworks are yours. Singapore isn't here because I couldn't find a public-domain artwork of it in that collection. Colour strips use flag colours as two or three thin bands, not the flag itself, which keeps clear of the "no official marks" rule. The wax seals are rendered as real wax: a pool with uneven edges, a raised rim, a stamped face with a monogram, tiny bubbles and a glossy highlight.
