Home, round 4

One friend's postcard at a time. Tap it to turn it over and read the back (each friend's country edition is pressed into the paper). Swipe up to flip to the next, quickly; swipe down to go back. Same Time and a roll passed to you appear as small pills at the top; tap them.

The backs are country editions: Japan (seigaiha waves, postal-code boxes), France (Correspondance / Adresse, Art Nouveau curves), Korea, Mexico (papel picado, pressed in) and Singapore (home: Peranakan tile). Turn each card over to compare. All received, so each has the sender's postmark.

Pick A, B or C, or a mix. Next: the screen map, with these screens in it.
