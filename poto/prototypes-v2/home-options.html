Home: four options

How your friends' day appears when you open Poto. Same friends, same postcards in each, so you compare only the idea. Each one is tappable. Swipe sideways for the next.

In all four: warm off-white, never pure white. Each postcard has the poto mark on the frame and the sender's signature stamp clipped on with a paper clip. The developing bar shows your own shots coming up overnight. Camera in the middle of the dock; the desk is your profile.

Tell me the letter you like (or "A's envelopes with C's stack"). Mixing is fine.
