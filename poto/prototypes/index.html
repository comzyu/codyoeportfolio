Poto · before any app code

Feel it first.

Ten moments of Poto to try on your phone. Tune each one until it feels right, then send the numbers back.

Tweak opens sliders for timing, easing and intensity. Copy values copies your settings as one line. Paste it back to Claude. Sound on toggles audio. The small black pill shows where the phone would give a haptic tap.
